t01 - fixture media, generated with ffmpeg
Date: 2026-11-02T15:20:29+00:00
ffmpeg version: ffmpeg version 8.1.2 Copyright (c) 2000-2026 the FFmpeg developers

Every fixture is a 5-second synthetic clip (test pattern video +
a 440 Hz tone) wearing an invented title. The titles follow the
naming shapes Plex expects: 'Name (Year)' for films, and
'Show/Season 01/Show - s01e01' for television.

$ plex-lab/make-fixtures.sh ~/workspace/plex-labwork/media
fixtures written under /home/hatch/workspace/plex-labwork/media
