t06 - a TV library: shows, seasons and episodes from folder shape
Date: 2026-11-02T15:32:22+00:00

Created exactly as the film library was, with the television
pairing (the bundled TVDB agent and the series scanner):

  POST /library/sections?name=TV&type=show
       &agent=com.plexapp.agents.thetvdb&scanner=Plex%20Series%20Scanner
       &language=en&location=.../media/tv
  -> 201 Created   (this is section 3)

The show took about 45 seconds to appear: the scan queues
behind the same analysis pass described in t05.

$ curl -s 'http://127.0.0.1:32400/library/sections/3/all' (the shows)
- show: The Walnut Street Bakery | guid: local://8 | ratingKey: 8 | seasons (childCount): 1 | episodes (leafCount): 2

$ curl -s 'http://127.0.0.1:32400/library/metadata/8/allLeaves' (the episodes)
-  The Walnut Street Bakery | season 1 episode 1 | title: Episode 1 | guid: local://10
     file: The Walnut Street Bakery - s01e01.mp4
-  The Walnut Street Bakery | season 1 episode 2 | title: Episode 2 | guid: local://11
     file: The Walnut Street Bakery - s01e02.mp4

Nobody told Plex this was season 1, episodes 1 and 2, in so
many words: the 'Season 01' folder and the s01e01 / s01e02 in
the filenames are the whole message, and the scanner read
them correctly. Episode titles are just the show name and
numbers - with an invented programme there is no online
episode list to borrow titles from, and the local:// guid
says no online record was attached.
