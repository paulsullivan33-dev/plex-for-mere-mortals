check that a library folder exists when you name it - the
section simply has nothing in it:

And failure 2's ending: with 'empty trash automatically after
every scan' at its default, the vanished film is not kept as a
greyed-out placeholder - one refresh and it is out of the
library entirely (size back to 3 above). The file's metadata
goes with it; restoring the file and refreshing would bring
it back as a fresh item.
